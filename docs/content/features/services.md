# Services

The Services feature provides content types to show the services and policies a council provides.

## Content types

- **Service landing page** — the top level section for each service
- **Service sub-landing page** — detail and links to specific pages within a service
- **Service page** — the basic page placed in a service hierarchy
- **Service status** — optional updates about service status

Other content types such as guides, directories, and step-by-step pages can be linked from service landing and sub-landing pages.

## Service landing pages

The top-level page for each council service. Shows:

- Service summary and body content
- Active service statuses
- Sub-landing page links
- Direct child pages

## Service sub-landing pages

Index pages that link to pages within a section of a service. Think of them as chapters — they group related pages together.

## Service pages

The most commonly used page type. Use for:

- Information pages
- Task starting points
- Policy documents

Choose a **guide** when content is related but not sequential. Choose **step by step** when the user follows a strict process.

## Service status

Give citizens updates about a service. Statuses have severity levels:

- Minor
- Major
- Announcement
- Notable person

Active statuses appear on the service landing page and can be shown site-wide via alert banners.

## Managing in admin

1. Create a **Service landing page** first
2. Add **Service sub-landing pages** for sections
3. Add **Service pages** under the service or sub-landing
4. Create **Service statuses** when needed and mark as active
